Kitchen

You manage to stumble your way into the kitchen. No sign of your sister.WHERE IS SHE? Who knows. You feel even worse. It feels like you’ve pulled a double shift at work; If you ever worked a day in your life. You are kind of hungry. Usually your nagging sister brings food along with her rude awakening. Even when your sister fails her assault on your relaxation, she always leaves warm food on the counter or in the fridge for when you really wish to stay unconscious. There is no food on the counter. You open the fridge to see a freshly made lobster and shrimp risotto. It was very unthoughtful of your sister to not warm it up.Maybe you should find her to complain again. You reluctantly take the plate and as the uncomfortable feeling of porcelain touches your fingers for the first time in what must be months, you ask:

“Leah, how long should I put this in the microwave for?”

There is no reply.
Maybe you should try to figure it out yourself. Or maybe you should go try and figure out what is wrong with Leah. You assumed that your sister was fixing Leah, which is why the food was cold. But now that both Leah and the food seem to be out of the ordinary, you question what your lazy worrywort of a sister is doing. All she does is nag and nag and nag. You are starting to miss Leah. Usually Leah would remind you to eat. She knows you better than you know yourself. Too bad she can’t bring you food when you wake up. You tried to convince your sister to install a bodily extension of Leah and even after one was sent to your house, your sister refused to install it. She is so lazy. You are starting to really worry about Leah. Your hunger is also going off the charts. The fact that the exhaustion is creeping in doesn't help. Try finding your sister to fix Leah or eat some food to stem your hunger.

what should you do?
